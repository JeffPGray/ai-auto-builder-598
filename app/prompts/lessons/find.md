## Finding Businesses
- **Email discovery yields (measured).** FB-linked candidates via `fb-page.js --brief` ≈ 1 in 3; search-found FB pages similar, but engines rate-limit after ~a dozen queries; IG business emails are unrecoverable logged-out; rescue leads' own sites ≈ 1 in 2+ — the richest source. Screening costs ~100x less than a build.
- **The unsampled categories held most of the supply.** In the test town behind Step 1's ~25% measurement, the missing majority was auto repair, tire shops, taquerias, bakeries, nail salons, laundromats. When a Layer 2 category converts for you, promote it into your own core sweep.
- **"No website" on Google Maps doesn't always mean no website.** Some businesses have a site but haven't linked it to their Maps listing, so gather's Step 0 runs a Yahoo search + domain-variation checks — always run it before gathering. Domain guessing alone isn't enough: businesses with long names often register shortened domains (e.g. "Northern Window Cleaning Services" might own `northernwindowcleaning.co.uk`, dropping "Services"), which the search catches because engines index them even when Maps doesn't link them. For long business names, try truncated domain variations (drop trailing generic words like "cleaning", "services", "solutions").
